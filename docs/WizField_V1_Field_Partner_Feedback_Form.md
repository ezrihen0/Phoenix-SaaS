# WizField V1.0 Field Partner Feedback

Please use this form to report your weekly feedback, workflow issues, blockers, confusion points, and feature value during the WizField V1.0 Field Partner program.

For urgent blockers, email service@wizfield.com immediately.

---

## Submission

Copy this template into your response and fill all required fields.

1. Partner name *(short answer, required)*
2. Business / organization name *(short answer, required)*
3. Email *(short answer, required)*
4. Workflow tested *(multiple choice, required)*  
   - Home  
   - Lead  
   - Job  
   - Estimate  
   - Invoice  
   - Settings  
   - Search  
   - AI  
   - Growth Center  
   - Other
5. What were you trying to do? *(paragraph, required)*
6. What did you expect to happen? *(paragraph, required)*
7. What actually happened? *(paragraph, required)*
8. Severity *(multiple choice, required)*  
   - P0 — Cannot use the product  
   - P1 — Blocks workflow  
   - P2 — Confusing or slow  
   - P3 — Polish / suggestion
9. Screenshot or video link *(short answer, optional)*
10. Value rating for this workflow *(linear scale 1–5, required)*  
    - 1 = Not valuable  
    - 5 = Very valuable
11. Would you use this workflow weekly? *(multiple choice, required)*  
    - Yes  
    - No  
    - Not sure
12. Why or why not? *(paragraph, required)*
13. What would make this worth paying for? *(paragraph, required)*
14. Did you find a workaround? *(paragraph, optional)*
15. Any other feedback? *(paragraph, optional)*
