# Gate 14 — Paid acquisition go / no-go

**Purpose:** Decide whether spend on ads, outbound, or partner acquisition is safe **now**, given product, trust, and measurement readiness.

## Go criteria (all must be true for **GO**)

1. **Positioning:** Public pages state PhoenixOS as a field-service operating system; no false “CRM-only” positioning that contradicts launch narrative.  
2. **Honest activation:** CTAs do not promise self-serve trial signup unless that path exists and is tested.  
3. **Legal:** Terms and Privacy are **final** (not draft placeholders).  
4. **Support:** Prospect and customer escalation paths are staffed or explicitly time-bounded.  
5. **Gate 12 / 13:** Formal **GO** / **PASS** recorded per dependency gate.  
6. **Measurement:** At minimum, landing CTA clicks and sign-in attempts can be observed (analytics script URL or equivalent).

## No-go triggers (any one → **NO-GO** for paid spend)

- Gate 12 not **GO** or isolation / acceptance criteria still failing.  
- Self-serve signup claimed in ads but **not** implemented (**prior-gate launch blocker** until fixed).  
- Terms/Privacy still draft placeholders.  
- No owner-approved support handling for inbound leads from ads.  
- Pricing page contradicts actual Clover / contract terms.
