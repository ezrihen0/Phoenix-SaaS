# WizField Marketing Growth Center
## Phase 1 Foundation Feature Card

Feature:
WizField Growth Center `/marketing` Phase 1 Foundation

Why now:
The `/marketing` roadmap is approved and locked. This phase creates the smallest safe execution slice so the product has a real authenticated foundation when the Growth Center workstream is opened.

Priority:
Approved future workstream, limited to Phase 1 only.

Business value:
Establish a tenant-safe Growth Center shell inside the office product without blending into launch activation or promising later marketing capabilities before they are ready.

Monetization value:
None in this phase. Monetization wiring remains out of scope until a later approved phase.

Scope:
- Add the authenticated `/marketing` route family for office roles only.
- Add a visible office navigation entry for Growth Center.
- Add a Phase 1 workspace shell with empty states for all approved sub-routes.
- Add a minimal backend `marketing` module with an organization-scoped foundation endpoint.
- Add initial organization-owned marketing persistence contracts only.

Done when:
- `/marketing` and its approved sub-routes load for office roles.
- The Growth Center is visible in office navigation.
- The backend exposes an organization-scoped foundation response for the Phase 1 workspace.
- Initial marketing entities are registered as organization-owned contracts.
- No Content Studio, AI generation, channel publishing, CRM intelligence, campaigns, automations, analytics, or monetization behavior is implemented.

Out of scope:
- Content Studio
- AI generation
- Channel publishing
- CRM opportunity detection
- Campaign builder behavior
- Marketing automations
- Analytics
- Monetization and entitlement wiring
