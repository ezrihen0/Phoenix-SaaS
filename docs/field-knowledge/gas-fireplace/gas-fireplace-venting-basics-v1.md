# Gas Fireplace — Venting Basics (V1)

## Purpose

Provide trade-level venting concepts for field staff without quoting exact clearances unless tied to an approved manual or authority.

## When to use this knowledge

- Questions about vent looks blocked, termination location, direct vent vs B-vent, snow/ debris at cap.
- Example: “Vent looks blocked.”

## Safe field checks

- Confirm vent type per nameplate/manual (direct vent co-axial, B-vent, etc.) — do not assume.
- Visual check of termination: blockage, damage, distance to openings (observe — do not cite inch counts without source).
- Check for obstructions at intake/exhaust paths visible from exterior.
- Interior: only inspect accessible vent connections per scope — no destructive disassembly.

## What evidence to collect

- Photos of termination and surrounding conditions.
- Customer report of recent storms, renovations, pest nests, etc.
- Model/serial for manual lookup by office or qualified gasfitter.

## What not to say

- Exact clearance distances (e.g., “12 inches to window”) without that model’s listed manual or local AHJ rule in hand.
- “Vent is code compliant” as a field certification.
- Instructions to reroute vent or alter listed components without qualified design.

## Customer explanation language

- “Venting has to match your fireplace model and local code — we document what we see and follow the manufacturer manual for any clearance questions.”
- “If the vent path looks blocked or damaged, we may need a licensed gas technician to verify before use.”

## Report-safe wording

- “Vent termination visually [clear/obstructed/damaged descriptor]. Model-specific clearances not verified on site without manufacturer documentation.”
- “Recommended clearing obstruction / qualified review per manufacturer and local authority.”

## Escalation conditions

- Blocked, crushed, or improperly terminated vent: advise do not operate until qualified review.
- Combustion symptoms plus vent concern: immediate safety escalation.

## Sales/service opportunity if applicable

- Vent cap cleaning or guard installation when compatible and in scope.
- Referral partnership with qualified gasfitter for corrective vent work.
