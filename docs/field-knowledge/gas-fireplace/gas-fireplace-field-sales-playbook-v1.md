# Gas Fireplace — Field Sales Playbook (V1)

## Purpose

Align field staff on helpful, low-pressure sales motions that respect safety, licensing, and manufacturer rules.

## When to use this knowledge

- “How do I sell service?” combined with maintenance opportunities on site.
- End-of-visit wrap-up when customer is satisfied with technical work.

## Safe field checks

- Only offer services tied to documented findings or manufacturer maintenance intervals.
- Confirm company pricebook / approved offerings — no custom gas work promises.
- Read customer readiness; safety issues override sales motion.

## What evidence to collect

- Documented findings that justify recommendation.
- Customer acknowledgment of optional services (yes/no/maybe follow-up).

## What not to say

- Fear-based or false-urgency gas safety claims.
- Competitor disparagement.
- Guarantees on safety, code, or perpetual performance.

## Customer explanation language

- “Based on what we documented today, these are the optional next steps manufacturers often recommend.”
- “No pressure — the important part is that anything safety-related gets handled by the right qualified person.”

## Report-safe wording

- “Presented optional [service]. Customer response: [accepted/declined/follow-up].”

## Escalation conditions

- If customer disputes safety: stop sales — supervisor + qualified gasfitter path.

## Sales/service opportunity if applicable

- Maintenance plan, cleaning, fan service, compatible accessories.
- Future visit scheduling for seasonal start-up / shut-down when company offers it.
- Referral credit to qualified gasfitter partner when appropriate (company policy).
