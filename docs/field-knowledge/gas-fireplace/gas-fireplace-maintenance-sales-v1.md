# Gas Fireplace — Maintenance Sales (V1)

## Purpose

Support ethical maintenance and service conversations tied to manufacturer schedules — without pressure tactics or safety guarantees.

## When to use this knowledge

- “What can I sell during a basic service?”
- “How do I sell service?”
- Annual maintenance, cleaning, inspection bundling discussions.

## Safe field checks

- Verify appliance has documented service history gap vs manufacturer interval (ask + visual).
- Identify factual needs: soot, fan noise, ignition reliability — document before recommending add-ons.
- Match offerings to company-approved services and technician scope.

## What evidence to collect

- Last service date, observed soot/debris, customer usage patterns.
- Manual-recommended maintenance interval (reference when available — do not invent intervals).

## What not to say

- “You’ll die without this package.”
- “This eliminates all CO risk.”
- Discount pressure or fear-based closing on gas safety.
- Selling gas pressure adjustments or component swaps outside scope.

## Customer explanation language

- “Manufacturers recommend periodic professional service to keep the fireplace running cleanly and reliably — here’s what we include in our maintenance visit.”
- “This documents today’s condition; it’s not a guarantee nothing else will ever arise.”

## Report-safe wording

- “Discussed manufacturer-recommended maintenance interval. Customer [accepted/declined] optional [service name].”

## Escalation conditions

- Customer has active safety symptoms: address safety first — do not prioritize upsell.
- Required repair beyond maintenance scope: qualified gasfitter referral.

## Sales/service opportunity if applicable

- Annual maintenance / cleaning package.
- Fan or glass gasket service when documented need exists.
- CO detector awareness (retail if company offers — no certification of detector).
