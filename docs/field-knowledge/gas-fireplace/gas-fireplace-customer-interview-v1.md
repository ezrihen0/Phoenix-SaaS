# Gas Fireplace — Customer Interview (V1)

## Purpose

Guide structured customer questions before hands-on work so technicians gather facts without over-promising or diagnosing beyond scope.

## When to use this knowledge

- Arrival at site, before opening panels or operating controls (unless emergency).
- Remote pre-visit triage (office/dispatcher) — questions only, no remote certification.

## Safe field checks

- Confirm appliance type (direct vent, B-vent, insert, etc.) as customer understands it — verify with nameplate when on site.
- Ask when symptoms started and whether they correlate with weather, renovations, or other appliance use.
- Ask about recent service, DIY work, or other trades near the fireplace/vent path.
- Confirm CO detectors are present and when last tested (customer report — you do not certify detector function unless in scope).

## What evidence to collect

- Symptom timeline, frequency, and triggers.
- Last service date and provider.
- Fuel type (natural gas / propane) as labeled on appliance.
- Customer goals: heat, ambiance, repair, upgrade, warranty concern.
- Photos permission for nameplate and vent termination (if company policy allows).

## What not to say

- “That sounds like a bad valve — I’ll fix it” before inspection.
- Blaming customer without facts.
- Promising same-day repair of gas components outside scope.

## Customer explanation language

- “I’ll ask a few questions so we inspect the right things and document accurately.”
- “Your answers help us follow the manufacturer’s checklist and local requirements.”

## Report-safe wording

- “Customer interview: reported [symptoms/concerns] since [timeframe]. Stated last service [date/unknown].”
- “Customer goal: [service/inspection/repair inquiry].”

## Escalation conditions

- Emergency symptoms (gas odor, CO alarm, illness): stop interview flow — follow safety-first escalation.
- Customer reports unqualified gas alterations: document and escalate before operational advice.

## Sales/service opportunity if applicable

- Maintenance plan discussion after factual interview — “manufacturer recommends periodic professional service.”
- Optional inspection documentation package when customer wants written record.
