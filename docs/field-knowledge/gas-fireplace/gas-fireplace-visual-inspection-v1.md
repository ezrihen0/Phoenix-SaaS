# Gas Fireplace — Visual Inspection (V1)

## Purpose

Define systematic visual inspection steps within technician scope — observation and documentation, not unlicensed gas work.

## When to use this knowledge

- On-site service or inspection visits after customer interview.
- When user asks “what should I check first?” on a gas fireplace.

## Safe field checks

- Exterior vent termination: visible blockage, damage, improper slope hints, vegetation, paint/caulk issues (visual only).
- Glass door: cracks, gasket condition, excessive soot (document — do not diagnose combustion chemistry).
- Firebox visible areas: debris, improper materials, signs of overheating (discoloration) — document only.
- Control compartment: only open per manufacturer access instructions; do not bypass interlocks.
- Fan/blower area: debris, obstruction (if accessible per manual).
- Nameplate: capture brand, model, serial when legible.

## What evidence to collect

- Dated photos of nameplate, vent cap/termination, glass condition, visible soot pattern.
- Written notes of abnormal observations (factual, neutral).
- Whether appliance matches listed installation type (insert vs built-in) per labels.

## What not to say

- “Vent clearance is X inches” without manufacturer manual or AHJ-approved source for that model.
- “Burner is fine” without scope-appropriate testing per manual.
- “No venting issues” from exterior look alone.

## Customer explanation language

- “I’m doing a visual check of what we can see safely and what the manufacturer expects us to document.”
- “Some issues need the manual and sometimes a licensed gasfitter — we’ll tell you clearly if that’s the case.”

## Report-safe wording

- “Visual inspection: vent termination [location/description]. Glass [condition]. Soot deposit [location/severity descriptor]. Nameplate [captured/unreadable].”
- “No invasive disassembly beyond manufacturer access panel per procedure.”

## Escalation conditions

- Damaged vent, melted components, heavy soot, improper materials in firebox: stop operation advice; escalate.
- Illegible/missing nameplate: escalate before parts or pressure recommendations.

## Sales/service opportunity if applicable

- Cleaning/service when soot or debris documented and within company offering.
- Glass gasket or fan service when manufacturer-compatible service is in scope.
