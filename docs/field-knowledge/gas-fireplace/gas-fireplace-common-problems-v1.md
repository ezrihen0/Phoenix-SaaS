# Gas Fireplace — Common Problems (V1)

## Purpose

Help technicians recognize common symptom patterns, document findings, and recommend appropriate next steps without unsupported diagnosis or unsafe fixes.

## When to use this knowledge

- Troubleshooting questions (won’t light, weak flame, black glass, odor, noise, fan issues).
- Example: “The glass is black — what does it mean?”

## Safe field checks

- Black/sooty glass: often indicates incomplete combustion or service interval issues — **document soot**, recommend manufacturer-specified cleaning/service; check vent path visually; escalate if heavy or recurring after service.
- Won’t ignite: verify power, pilot/ignition per manual; do not bypass safety circuits.
- Weak or uneven flame: observe only; do not adjust gas pressure or air shutters unless licensed and per manual.
- Odor when running: distinguish new-appliance off-gassing vs ongoing combustion concern — when in doubt, escalate.
- Fan not running: check power, switch, debris — per manual; do not override limits.

## What evidence to collect

- Symptom description, duration, photos of flame/glass (if safe to capture).
- Service history and fuel type.
- Whether problem occurs at startup, steady state, or shutdown.

## What not to say

- Definitive root cause without manual-backed testing in scope.
- “Just clean the glass and it’s fixed” when venting/combustion may be involved.
- Part numbers or replacements without verified model compatibility.

## Customer explanation language

- “Soot on the glass often means the fireplace needs proper cleaning and a service check — we’ll document what we see and follow the manufacturer’s steps.”
- “Some causes need a qualified gas technician to test safely — we won’t guess.”

## Report-safe wording

- “Customer reported [symptom]. Observed [soot/flame/odor descriptor]. Recommended [service scope / qualified gasfitter review] per manufacturer requirements.”

## Escalation conditions

- Heavy soot after recent service, CO symptoms, gas odor, flame rollout: safety-first escalation.
- Repeated ignition failures: qualified gasfitter / manufacturer support path.

## Sales/service opportunity if applicable

- Manufacturer-scheduled maintenance and glass cleaning service.
- Fan or ignition component service **only** when model-verified and in company scope.
