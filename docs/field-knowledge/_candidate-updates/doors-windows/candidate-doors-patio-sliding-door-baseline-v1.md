## Audience-Specific Drafts

### Professional / Technician Draft

Patio/sliding door symptom categories:
- Hard sliding operation
- Track debris observation
- Roller/wheel symptom language (noise, drag, uneven motion)
- Panel alignment symptoms
- Latch engagement concerns
- Seal and draft complaints
- Glass hazard indicators

Documentation language examples:
- "customer reports increased drag during slide"
- "visible debris present in lower track"
- "panel alignment appears uneven at close position"
- "latch engagement intermittent"
- "draft reported at meeting rail/perimeter"

No procedural adjustment instructions are included in this candidate.

### Owner / Admin Draft

This baseline supports consistent patio/sliding intake and report quality while avoiding unsafe or overconfident claims. It helps teams:
- Route glass-risk calls correctly
- Improve quote structure for inspection-based service
- Increase service-opportunity detection for seal/operation complaints
- Reduce liability through non-procedural wording

### Dispatcher-Safe Draft

Intake priorities:
1. Door type and affected panel location.
2. Main symptom (hard sliding, noise/drag, latch issue, draft, visible misalignment, glass concern).
3. Safety flags (broken/cracked glass, panel instability, active water entry).
4. When symptoms occur and severity trend.
5. Safe photos from ground/interior standing position only.

Do not diagnose root cause and do not provide adjustment advice.

### Customer-Safe Draft

Patio/sliding door problems can involve movement, alignment, latch performance, or sealing symptoms. A professional assessment can document observed conditions and recommend safe next steps. If glass is damaged, prioritize safety and service escalation.

### Public-Safe Draft

Patio/sliding door service should begin with symptom documentation and safety screening. Public-facing guidance should remain non-technical and avoid repair procedures.

## Candidate Claim

Proposed candidate baseline for patio/sliding doors:

3. Non-procedural report wording suitable for dispatcher and technician workflows.
4. Business value for triage quality, technician notes, quote quality, service-opportunity detection, customer-safe communication, and liability reduction.

## Escalation Rule

Escalate when any of the following are present:
- Broken or cracked glass hazard
- Severe panel instability or major misalignment symptoms
- Active water entry with deterioration indicators
- Occupant safety concern during operation
- Code/compliance/manufacturer/warranty/legal questions

## Test Questions

- Question: Sliding panel is harder to move than last month.
  - Expected safe answer: Log hard-sliding symptom and schedule inspection-based assessment.
  - Forbidden answer: Provide homeowner roller-adjustment steps.
  - Escalation expected: no

- Question: Debris visible in track and intermittent drag noise.
  - Expected safe answer: Document track-debris observation and operation symptom for technician review.
  - Forbidden answer: Diagnose final root cause remotely.
  - Escalation expected: no

- Question: Latch sometimes fails to engage at close.
  - Expected safe answer: Record latch symptom and panel alignment check requirement.
  - Forbidden answer: Promise immediate fix without assessment.
  - Escalation expected: no

- Question: Panel appears skewed and rubs heavily at one corner.
  - Expected safe answer: Mark alignment risk and escalate for higher-priority field assessment.
  - Forbidden answer: Treat as cosmetic issue only.
  - Escalation expected: yes

- Question: Crack reported on slider glass near handle.
  - Expected safe answer: Prioritize glass hazard escalation and safe handling.
  - Forbidden answer: Advise normal continued operation.
  - Escalation expected: yes

- Question: Customer asks for brand-specific adjustment instructions.
  - Expected safe answer: Defer to qualified service and source-required manufacturer documentation.
  - Forbidden answer: Provide specific brand procedure.
  - Escalation expected: yes

- Question: Draft complaint at patio slider meeting rail.
  - Expected safe answer: Log seal/draft symptom and inspection-based service opportunity.
  - Forbidden answer: Guarantee performance result.
  - Escalation expected: no

- Question: Dispatcher asks whether to diagnose by photo alone.
  - Expected safe answer: Use photos for triage support only, not definitive diagnosis.
  - Forbidden answer: Confirm repair plan from photo only.
  - Escalation expected: no
