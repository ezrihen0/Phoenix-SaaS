## Audience-Specific Drafts

### Professional / Technician Draft

Cold-climate observation categories (symptoms only):
- Seasonal draft complaints
- Freeze/thaw-related sticking symptoms
- Interior surface condensation complaints as symptom reports only
- Fogging between panes / IGU fogging complaints as separate symptom reports only
- Seal compression concerns in low temperatures
- Threshold/sweep complaints during winter periods
- Winter water/ice observation language around openings
- Timing questions tied to weather events and temperature swings

Required boundaries:
- Candidate only, not jurisdiction guidance
- Not energy code guidance
- Not building code guidance
- Not manufacturer guidance
- Cold-climate observations are not permit/code rules
- Do not treat interior surface condensation and fogging between panes as the same condition.
- Do not diagnose failed glass seal, ventilation issue, humidity issue, or product defect from cold-weather timing alone.

Use wording like:
- "symptom appears weather-correlated per customer report"
- "cause not confirmed from seasonality alone"

### Owner / Admin Draft

Operational value:
- improves seasonal dispatcher triage quality
- improves technician note context with weather timing
- improves report wording and quote sequencing in winter months
- improves service-opportunity detection without overclaiming cause
- improves customer-safe communication and liability reduction

### Dispatcher-Safe Draft

Cold-climate intake questions:
1. When did symptom start relative to colder weather?
2. Is symptom constant or tied to freeze/thaw swings?
3. Is moisture/ice visible near opening?
4. Any broken glass or safety concern?
5. Can customer share safe-location photos?

Do not diagnose code, legal status, or definitive cause from weather timing.

### Customer-Safe Draft

In colder climates, symptoms can change with weather and temperature swings. Reporting timing details helps your service team triage more accurately, but final cause should be confirmed by professional assessment.

### Public-Safe Draft

Cold weather can influence doors/windows symptoms. Public-safe messaging should stay high-level and avoid compliance claims or repair instruction.
