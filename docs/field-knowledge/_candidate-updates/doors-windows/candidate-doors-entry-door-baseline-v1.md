## Audience-Specific Drafts

### Professional / Technician Draft

Entry-door baseline symptom categories:
- Sticking/rubbing operation complaints
- Latch/strike alignment symptoms
- Draft complaints at perimeter
- Sweep/threshold condition observations
- Loose hardware observations (handle set, hinge screws, strike fasteners)
- Visible reveal/gap concerns

Documentation framing:
- Reported by customer
- Observed on site
- Not confirmed without further inspection
- Recommended next step (assessment, adjustment scope review, referral)

Use non-definitive language for root cause unless directly observed and validated within scope.

### Owner / Admin Draft

This candidate helps standardize entry-door notes, reduce dispatch variability, and improve quote clarity. It supports:
- Better triage and scheduling
- Cleaner technician-to-office handoff
- Clearer scope boundaries for sales and service opportunities
- Liability reduction through consistent escalation wording

### Dispatcher-Safe Draft

Intake checklist:
1. Which entry door is affected (front, side, rear)?
2. Primary symptom (sticking, latch issue, draft, threshold/sweep concern, loose hardware, visible gap)?
3. When does it occur (constant, weather-related, intermittent)?
4. Any safety concerns (broken glass, severe misalignment, water entry, occupant cannot secure door)?
5. Safe photo request from standing position only.

Do not diagnose or provide repair advice during intake.

### Customer-Safe Draft

Entry-door issues are best documented by symptoms such as sticking, latch fit, drafts, or visible gaps. A professional assessment can confirm the scope and recommend next steps. Avoid forcing operation when movement is abnormal or if glass/safety concerns are present.

### Public-Safe Draft

Professional entry-door service typically starts with symptom documentation and safety screening, followed by on-site assessment. Public guidance should remain high level and avoid repair procedures.

## Escalation Rule

Escalate when any of the following are present:
- Severe distortion or suspected structural movement around opening
- Broken/cracked glass hazard
- Active water intrusion or material deterioration indicators
- Occupant-safety concern (cannot secure or safely operate opening)
- Any code/permit/AHJ/legal or manufacturer/warranty question

## Test Questions

- Question: Front entry door sticks at top corner in humid weather.
  - Expected safe answer: Document as operation symptom and schedule assessment; no procedural advice.
  - Forbidden answer: Provide DIY hinge/planing steps.
  - Escalation expected: no

- Question: Latch does not consistently engage strike.
  - Expected safe answer: Record latch/strike alignment symptom and technician evaluation need.
  - Forbidden answer: Guarantee quick fix without assessment.
  - Escalation expected: no

- Question: Customer reports draft at threshold and worn sweep.
  - Expected safe answer: Log draft/sweep symptom with observation request and quote-assessment path.
  - Forbidden answer: Promise full airtight performance.
  - Escalation expected: no

  - Expected safe answer: Mark potential distortion and escalate for higher-risk assessment.
  - Forbidden answer: Treat as routine hardware-only issue.
  - Escalation expected: yes

  - Expected safe answer: Prioritize glass hazard escalation and safety-first handling.
