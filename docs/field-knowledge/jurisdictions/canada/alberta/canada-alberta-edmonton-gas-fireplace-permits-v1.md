## AI-safe summary (source-backed, cautious)

Per City of Edmonton pages at verification:

- A **gas permit** is required under the provincial **Safety Codes Act** when **installing, altering, or relocating** gas equipment including—listed on the City page—**fireplaces**, furnaces, water heaters, piping, ranges, and similar equipment.
- A permit must be **applied for and issued before starting work** and before requesting inspection.
- Apply via City processes (e.g. **self-serve portal** referenced on City pages) with required forms such as **Gas Permit Details for New Gas Appliance Application** for new appliance work.
- **Homeowner gas permit** (renovation/re-test): for **single detached house** only; homeowner must **own and occupy** and **perform work themselves** per City page. **New home** gas work must be by a **licensed mechanical contractor**.
- **Semi-detached, row housing, rental, or non-owner-occupied** scenarios: **licensed mechanical contractor** must apply (City homeowner/trade guidance on related pages).
- Gas permits are often combined with **Home Improvement Permit** or **New House Permit** when part of a larger project; standalone gas permit when project is **gas work only**—City advises not to apply separately when trade permits should be part of a building permit project.
- **Contractors must be licensed** with the City of Edmonton.
- Inspection may request photos/documentation (e.g. piping under test, connections, exterior vent terminations, clearances to combustibles for fireplaces)—per City new gas appliance PDF; field staff should not invent clearance values.
- Work on **detached garages/sheds** with gas may require trade permits as part of home improvement permitting (see Detached Garage guide cross-reference).

Limitations / guardrails:

- This pack does **not** determine whether **routine service, maintenance, or diagnostic work** requires a permit.
- Permit requirements are **scope-dependent** and must be verified with the **City of Edmonton / AHJ** before advising the customer.
- This pack does **not** state fees, approval timelines, or inspection outcomes.

## What AI may say

- **Edmonton** treats gas fireplace **installation, alteration, or relocation** as **gas equipment work** that typically requires a **gas permit** under the Safety Codes Act framework described by the City.
- For routine service or maintenance, this pack does **not** provide a permit exemption; verify with **City of Edmonton / AHJ**.
- Permit and inspection requirements depend on **scope**, **housing type** (single detached vs. other), **owner-occupier** status, and whether work is bundled with a building/home improvement permit.
- **Verify** with **City of Edmonton** and the **AHJ** before work; use licensed contractors when City pages require it.
- For inspection documentation, refer to City forms/PDF—not improvised clearance rules.

## Escalation rule

Escalate to **City of Edmonton** permit services, **licensed mechanical/gas contractor**, or company compliance when:

- Install, alter, relocate, or extend gas piping to a fireplace.
- Property is not single-detached owner-occupied (homeowner permit limitations).
- New construction vs. renovation permit path is unclear.
- Customer wants to start work before permit issuance (City: begin only after issued permits).
