## Purpose

## AI-safe summary

- **Alberta** establishes and interprets gas codes and standards under the **Safety Codes Act** (Municipal Affairs gas discipline per Alberta.ca).
- **In force (declared on Alberta.ca):** CSA B149.1:25 (natural gas and propane installation), CSA B149.2:25 (propane storage and handling), and related B149 series editions—**declared in force March 1, 2026** per Alberta.ca at time of verification. Prior editions (e.g. B149.1:20) were in force through February 28, 2026.
- Gas-fired appliance installation is addressed by the adopted CSA B149 series and regulations; **field work must follow manufacturer instructions**, the **applicable declared code edition**, and the **local AHJ** (city/municipality).
- **Qualified/certified persons** perform gas fitting work as required by Alberta law, certification, and scope of work—this pack does not define licence classes.
- This knowledge pack is an **AI routing summary**, not a code book or legal opinion.

## What AI may say

- Gas fireplace work in Alberta must be treated as **fuel-burning appliance work** under provincial safety codes.
- Requirements depend on **scope** (service vs. new install vs. alteration vs. gas piping) and must be verified against the **manufacturer manual**, **applicable Alberta-declared gas code** (see Alberta.ca for in-force edition), **permit authority**, and **AHJ**.
- When the in-force code edition is uncertain for a job date, direct the user to Alberta.ca and a qualified gasfitter rather than guessing edition transitions.
- Municipal permit processes (Calgary, Edmonton, etc.) may add requirements beyond this provincial baseline.

## What AI must not say

- “This installation is code compliant.”
- “No permit is required.”
- “Any person can perform gas work.”
- Exact clearances, pipe sizes, or vent dimensions **without** manufacturer manual or official code excerpt in context.
- That this pack supersedes City of Calgary, City of Edmonton, or other AHJ rules.

## Escalation rule

Escalate to a **qualified Alberta gasfitter**, company compliance lead, or **local AHJ** when:

- Installation, relocation, alteration, or gas piping is involved.
- Code edition, permit need, or compliance is in question.
- Customer requests certification or “sign-off” beyond field scope.
- Municipality is unknown—do not assume Calgary rules apply in Edmonton or vice versa.
