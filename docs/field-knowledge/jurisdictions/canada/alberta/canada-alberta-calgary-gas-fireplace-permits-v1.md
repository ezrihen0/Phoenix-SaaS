## AI-safe summary (source-backed, cautious)

Per City of Calgary pages at verification:

- **Gas fireplace work may require one or more permit types** depending on who performs which parts of the installation (homeowner vs. qualified contractor).
- **Contractor’s gas permit** — City of Calgary states this permit can include the **fireplace, venting, and gas connection** when a **qualified gas fitter** applies and performs the work (single permit for full installation by qualified gas fitter).
- **Contractor’s gas fireplace installation permit** — Includes installation of **new fireplaces, gas inserts, and related venting**; does **not** include the **gas line connection** that serves the appliance (per heating/cooling and trades pages).
- **Homeowner’s gas fireplace installation permit** — Available when the **homeowner** installs a fireplace insert themselves in their home; addresses vent/mantel clearances per City page; does **not** include **gas line connection**—connection requires a **qualified, licensed gas fitter** with a separate **contractor’s gas permit**.
- **Two permits** may be required when the fireplace/venting installer is **not** a qualified gas fitter and gas connection is separate; **one permit** when a qualified gas fitter does the **entire** installation including piping/connections.
- **Homeowners generally cannot obtain a standard gas permit** on their house unless they are a **first- or second-class gasfitter (journeyman)**; otherwise a **qualified licensed contractor** must pull the gas permit.
- Trade contractors need **City of Calgary** qualification, business licence, and appropriate trade certification to apply (trade contractor pages).
- **Gas lines to a fireplace** may require a **separate gas permit**; City inspection pages note gas lines must be installed by an **Alberta-certified journeyman gas fitter**.
- If unsure whether a permit is needed, City directs residents to contact the **Planning Services Centre** (403-268-5311 per trades page).

This pack does **not** list fees, inspection schedules, or form numbers—verify on City pages at time of application.

## What AI may say

- Permit handling in **Calgary** depends on **scope**: appliance/insert/venting versus **gas connection**, and **who** performs the work (homeowner vs. qualified gas fitter vs. other contractor).
- **Gas connection** work must follow the **proper qualified/licensed process** and typically requires a **contractor’s gas permit** issued to a qualified gas fitter—not the homeowner fireplace permit alone.
- When a **qualified gas fitter** performs the **full** installation (fireplace, venting, piping/connections), City guidance describes completing work under a **contractor’s gas permit**.
- **Confirm** current requirements with **City of Calgary** and the **responsible qualified gasfitter** before starting work.
- Alberta provincial gas code baseline still applies; this pack is **municipal permit routing** only.

## What AI must not say

- “Calgary never requires a permit.”
- “A fireplace permit always covers the gas connection.”

## Escalation rule

Escalate to **City of Calgary Planning Services Centre**, the **permit-holding qualified gasfitter**, or company compliance when:

- Scope includes new install, insert, venting change, or gas piping/connection.
- Homeowner asks to DIY gas connection.
- Permit type (gas vs. gas fireplace vs. homeowner fireplace) is unclear.
- Work was started without confirmed issued permits (City states homeowners remain responsible for penalties even if a contractor assured permits were unnecessary).
